From pomace to wallet

  1. Collect

    Pomace is picked up at the press, right after the harvest.

  2. Dry & grind

    Skins and seeds are dried, then milled into a fine powder.

  3. Bind

    The powder is bound with a natural binder into supple sheets.

  4. Finish

    Vaud artisans cut, stitch and finish your piece by hand.
